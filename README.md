# 🌍 *Orb: On the Movements of the Earth*

> *“The Earth moves — even if the world refuses to believe it.”*

---

## Overview  

**Orb: On the Movements of the Earth** is a *historical-science drama* anime about the search for truth in a world ruled by fear and ignorance.  
It highlights the courage of thinkers who challenge powerful beliefs to uncover scientific reality.  

---

## Key Themes  
1. **Truth vs. Belief**  
2. *Freedom of Thought*  
3. ~~Silence under oppression~~
